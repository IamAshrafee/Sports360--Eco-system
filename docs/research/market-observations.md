# Bangladesh Market Observations

Status: Preliminary desk research
Last reviewed: 2026-07-24

These observations help shape hypotheses. Product/vendor marketing claims are
not independent proof of market size, adoption, or customer satisfaction.

## Observed local product activity

Several Bangladesh-focused products publicly position themselves around sports
venue discovery, real-time slot availability, online payment, and venue-owner
management:

### Turfly

Public materials describe:

- Venue discovery and real-time availability
- Football, cricket, badminton, swimming, and other categories
- Booking and digital payment
- Venue-owner value around centralized management and no-show reduction
- A growing venue network in Dhaka

Sources:

- [Turfly website](https://turfly.app/)
- [Turfly Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ternary.turfly)

### TurfLet

Public materials describe:

- Live slots and verified venues
- Owner management of bookings and walk-ins
- Payments, payouts, and occupancy
- Bangladesh-focused payment methods

Source:

- [TurfLet](https://www.turflet.app/)

### TurfBook

Public materials describe:

- Public pages and OTP booking
- bKash/Nagad payment-information submission
- Prevention of duplicate bookings
- Operator booking, collection, rental, package, tournament, and reporting tools
- Support beyond football for pools, courts, tables, and lanes

Source:

- [TurfBook](https://turfbook.vip/)

### Other emerging listings

Other public sites include:

- [Turffin](https://www.turffin.com/)
- [Top Turf](https://topturfbd.com/)
- [KhelbiNaki](https://www.khelbinakibd.com/)

Their existence indicates active experimentation around venue booking in
Bangladesh. It does not establish their operating scale.

## Payment-provider observations

Bangladesh payment gateways publicly advertise combinations of cards, mobile
financial services, merchant reporting, and APIs:

- [SSLCOMMERZ](https://sslcommerz.com/payment-gateway/)
- [aamarPay](https://aamarpay.com/)
- [shurjoPay](https://shurjopay.com.bd/)

Current provider claims, fees, supported channels, onboarding documents, refund
behavior, recurring support, sub-merchant capability, and settlement terms must
be verified directly before selection.

## Implications

1. The booking problem is visible enough to attract multiple local products.
2. “Find and book a turf” alone is unlikely to be durable differentiation.
3. Phone/message/manual payment and owner operations remain central claims in
   local products.
4. A business-management-first position deserves validation.
5. Gateway abstraction is prudent because provider terms and capability change.
6. Competitive research should include accessible live product trials and
   organic usage evidence, not only landing pages.
