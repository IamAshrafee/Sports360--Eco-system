# Public Evidence Register

Status: Phase 1 desk research
Last reviewed: 2026-07-24

## Reading rules

- **Observed fact:** Directly visible on the cited public page.
- **Vendor claim:** A company describes its own product, scale, or customer
  problem.
- **Inference:** Our interpretation; not directly proven.
- **Confidence:** Confidence that the cited page supports the observation, not
  confidence that the whole market behaves the same way.

Public pages can change and may contain inaccurate or promotional content.

## Venue-operation evidence

### EV-001: Time-based prices, duration choice, advance, and WhatsApp fallback

Source: [Prime Arena public booking page](https://primearenabd.com/book)

Observed facts:

- The page offers one-, two-, three-, and four-hour duration choices.
- Hourly start times are grouped into morning, midday, afternoon, prime, and
  late-night price bands.
- Displayed test/retrieved rates vary by time.
- Payment choices include full payment and a 30% advance with remaining amount
  at the venue.
- The page can disable online payment and direct customers to WhatsApp.
- Late-night start times extend beyond midnight.

Inferences:

- Time-band pricing and partial confirmation are concrete product needs.
- A booking page may coexist with manual-message completion.
- Exact timestamp and operational-day separation is justified.
- Fixed start times can coexist with multi-hour duration selection.

Assumptions affected: A-002, A-005, A-006
Confidence: High for this venue page; low for market prevalence

### EV-002: Fixed 90-minute products and day/time pricing

Source: [Top Turf](https://topturfbd.com/)

Observed facts:

- Public pricing is displayed as 90-minute products.
- Sunrise, midday, and night periods have distinct prices.
- Thursday–Saturday and Sunday–Wednesday pricing are distinguished.
- Refreshments and playing kits are presented as included items.
- The venue also markets events and private rooftop use.

Inferences:

- Fixed slots are not necessarily limited to 60 minutes.
- Included services belong in Offering/add-on snapshot design.
- Day-of-week and time-band pricing is justified.
- Private events are a real adjacent pressure but remain future scope.

Assumptions affected: A-005
Confidence: High for this page; low for market prevalence

### EV-003: One-hour standard, extension, cancellation window, events

Source: [Timeout Turf](https://www.timeout.com.bd/)

Observed facts:

- The FAQ describes a standard one-hour booking.
- Extra time is described as dependent on availability.
- Booking is described as date/time selection followed by payment.
- The FAQ describes full refund when cancelled at least 24 hours in advance.
- Events and tournament bookings are offered through customized contact.

Inferences:

- Conflict-safe extension and configurable cancellation windows are justified.
- Ordinary slot booking and event inquiry are different workflows.

Assumptions affected: A-005
Confidence: High for this venue's published policy

### EV-004: Field-size offerings and price variation

Source: [BookMyBattle venue listing](https://www.bookmybattle.com/turfs)

Observed facts:

- Public listings include division/district/area-style location filtering.
- Venue cards display different field sizes such as 4v4, 5v5, 6v6, and 7v7.
- Average hourly prices vary across listed venues.
- Listings include Dhaka and Chattogram locations.

Inferences:

- One resource may expose different customer-facing play formats.
- Activity/offering and physical resource should remain separate.
- Location, field size, and transparent price are useful marketplace metadata.

Assumptions affected: A-005
Confidence: High for listing content; vendor inventory/scale not independently verified

### EV-005: Multi-location and multi-sport public structure

Source: [GAME ON](https://gameon.bd/)

Observed facts:

- The site presents Bogura and Khulna locations.
- It exposes location, sport, date, and slot-search concepts.
- Public venue information includes football/futsal formats and badminton.
- It describes venue-specific pricing, availability, and facilities.

Inferences:

- Multi-venue businesses exist as a credible architecture pressure.
- Venue scope, location selection, and business-level branding should remain
  separate.

Assumptions affected: A-013
Confidence: High for the public structure

## Product and competitor evidence

### EV-006: Detailed operator workflow for Bangladesh facilities

Source: [TurfBook](https://turfbook.vip/)

Vendor claims:

- Facility setup includes resources, weekly slots/sessions, rates, minimum
  payment, accessories, and blocked dates.
- Customer booking uses a public page, Bangladesh phone OTP, hold timer, and
  bKash/Nagad payment-detail submission.
- Admins/managers handle direct bookings, payments, corrections, accessories,
  and dues.
- The system groups verified cash/bKash/Nagad collections back to bookings.
- It advertises expenses, packages, tournaments, reports, exports, staff
  controls, and customer/mobile restrictions.

Inferences:

- Our booking, partial-payment, collection, resource block, rental, report, and
  role models align with repeated local product requirements.
- OTP plus hold timer is not only a generic international pattern.
- Broader future capabilities are locally relevant but should remain outside
  the MVP until justified.

Assumptions affected: A-001, A-002, A-006, A-015
Confidence: High that the vendor makes these claims; product adoption unverified

### EV-007: Owner operations positioned separately from marketplace growth

Sources:

- [TurfLet owner page](https://www.turflet.app/for-owners)
- [TurfLet about page](https://www.turflet.app/about)

Vendor claims:

- Owners need one calendar for online, walk-in, phone, and social bookings.
- Owner tools include Today/dashboard, turfs/fields, booking control, revenue,
  payouts, analytics, staff, and reports.
- Operations are positioned as a required backbone while marketplace and
  WhatsApp automation are optional growth modules.
- Registration, verification, listing/configuration, operation, and growth form
  an owner onboarding sequence.

Inferences:

- Business-management-first positioning has a current local analogue and is not
  an invented category.
- Operations and marketplace can remain separate entitlements/business models.

Assumptions affected: A-001, A-002, A-007, A-008, A-015
Confidence: High for vendor positioning; customer outcomes unverified

## What this evidence supports

Desk-supported:

- Bangladesh-facing products repeatedly describe fragmented calls/messages/
  paper and the need for one booking calendar.
- Fixed slots, time-band prices, partial/advance payment, late-night operation,
  manual-message fallback, and configurable cancellation exist in public venue
  flows.
- Venue operations, customer booking, and marketplace growth can be separated.
- Multi-location operation is a credible future pressure.

Not validated:

- Frequency across the whole market
- Staff adoption
- Owner trust
- Willingness to pay
- Correct price
- Which notification/language is essential
- Competitor active usage, revenue, or customer satisfaction
