# Open Questions

These questions are intentionally unresolved. Answers should be supported by
public research, scenario analysis, organic behavior, provider research, or
professional advice.

## Customer and market

- Which city and venue type should the first detailed archetype prioritize?
- What exact booking volume creates willingness to pay?
- Which business title and terminology are most natural in Bangla and English?
- How much owner value comes from control versus additional online demand?
- What monthly and annual prices are credible?

## Booking operations

- How long should an online temporary hold last?
- Which pending bookings reserve inventory and for how long?
- Is manual approval common enough for the pilot?
- What grace period defines late arrival and no-show?
- How frequently do venues extend play?
- Which cancellation/rescheduling policies are common enough for templates?
- How often do initial customers require flexible-duration slots?

## Customer identity

- Should OTP be required for every public booking or only higher-risk actions?
- What evidence is required to claim historical guest bookings?
- How common are shared phone numbers?
- Do pilot venues need anonymous walk-ins?
- What customer information must reception staff see?

## Payments and finance

- Which manual payment fields are necessary for reliable MFS reconciliation?
- Which gateway best fits venue-owned settlement and multi-tenant integration?
- Which venues already have merchant accounts?
- When is a future booking balance considered due versus overdue?
- Is basic expense tracking required before pilot launch?
- What receipt/invoice and Bangladesh tax requirements apply?

## Notification and language

- Which transactional channel has the highest delivery and customer trust?
- Is SMS required for the first pilot or can shareable links suffice?
- Which screens require Bangla first?
- What terminology should be bilingual?

## SaaS and pilot

- Length and terms of the organic beta period
- Trial/grace/retention durations
- Initial plan packaging and limits
- Support expectations and response boundaries
- Data-import method for upcoming bookings
- Conditions for moving from one to several active venues

## Future horizon priorities

- Which H1 capability most directly improves willingness to pay after the pilot?
- Which future resource configurations occur frequently enough to influence the
  first data model?
- Are membership/package sales more valuable than marketplace acquisition for
  initial venues?
- Which organized-sports segment—tournaments, coaching, or academies—is the
  strongest adjacent market?
- At what venue/network scale does marketplace discovery become liquid?
- Which future capability creates unacceptable legal, financial, privacy, or
  support responsibility for a solo founder?

## Legal, privacy, and security

- Bangladesh privacy notice, consent, retention, and deletion requirements
- Required records for financial transactions and invoices
- Child/guardian requirements for youth activities
- Platform responsibility for marketplace listings and disputes
- Merchant-of-record and payout implications
- Incident-data access and retention

## Technical decisions deliberately not made

- Frontend framework and application topology
- Backend framework and API style
- Database and multi-tenancy implementation
- Authentication/OTP provider
- Payment gateway
- SMS/notification provider
- Cloud, region, deployment, observability, and backup providers
- Monorepo/package management strategy
- Native app strategy

These should follow detailed workflows, acceptance criteria, data modeling, and
non-functional requirements.
